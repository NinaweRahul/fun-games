Cake Stack Duel

Two bakers, two wobbly towers. Stack fast, stack neat, and do not topple.

How to play

Each baker has a tray that swings side to side. Tap to drop the cake layer onto your tower.

Anything hanging over the edge gets sliced off, so your layers shrink. Land one almost perfectly and it grows back a little.

Every 4th layer carries a cherry. Drop that layer perfectly to score 2 bonus layers. Miss, and the cherry rolls away.

If a layer misses your tower completely, it topples and you lose. The first baker to stack 15 layers wins. The tray gets faster as you climb.

Berry (left): A key, the pink button, or tap the left side

Mint (right): L key, the green button, or tap the right side
