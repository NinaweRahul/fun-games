Monkey Fruit Fight

Two monkeys. One jungle. Lots of flying fruit.

How to play

Drag anywhere to pull back like a slingshot, then let go to throw. The farther you pull, the harder the throw. Dots show the start of the arc.

Pick a fruit below the board. Banana is your all-rounder and never runs out. Coconut has a big blast but shorter range (2 each). Berries split into three at the top of the arc (2 each).

The wind pushes fruit sideways. Every hit costs a heart, even on your own monkey. Fruit also blows craters in the ground, so the hills change as you play.

Lose all 3 hearts and you lose. Kiki (teal) goes first, then Bobo (coral). Playing on one screen? Pass the device back and forth.
