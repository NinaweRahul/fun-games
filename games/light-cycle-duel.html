Light Cycle Duel

Two bikes, one arena. Last rider standing wins.

Steer north, east, south and west with the arrow keys or W A S D, or use the joystick.

How to play

Your bike never stops and can't reverse. Point it north, south, east or west to steer it around the arena. Hitting a wall or any light trail blows you up, and the other player wins. The bikes get faster over time.

Player 1, cyan: arrow keys. Up is north, down is south, left is west, right is east.

Player 2, orange: W (north), A (west), S (south), D (east)

On a phone, use the joysticks at the bottom: orange on the left, cyan on the right. Push a stick up, down, left or right to head that way. Pushing straight back the way you came does nothing.
