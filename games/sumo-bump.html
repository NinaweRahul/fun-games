Sumo Bump

Two penguins. One shrinking ice floe. Shove your rival into the sea.

How to play

Slide around the ice and bump your rival off the edge. The floe shrinks as the round goes on. Fall in the water and you lose the round. First to 2 rounds wins.

Dash is a big shove, but you slide far afterwards, so do not overshoot the edge. It needs a moment to recharge.

Pom (orange): W A S D to move, Space to dash

Pip (blue): arrow keys to move, Enter to dash

On a phone, use the joysticks and Dash buttons at the bottom: orange on the left, blue on the right.
