Frog Pond

Two hungry frogs. One buzzing pond. Time your tongue and snatch the flies.

How to play

Each frog's aim swings back and forth. Watch the dotted line, and hit your big tongue button when it points at a fly.

A regular fly is worth 1 point. A shiny golden fly is worth 3 but zips around fast and does not stay long.

If your tongue misses, your frog gets sleepy and cannot shoot for a moment, so do not just mash the button.

Both frogs can go for the same fly. Whoever shoots first gets it. Most points after 60 seconds wins.

Lily (left, pink): A key or the pink button

Moss (right, blue): L key or the blue button
