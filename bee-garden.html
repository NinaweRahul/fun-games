Bee Garden

Two bees, one garden. Paint more flowers than your rival before time runs out.

How to play

Fly over a flower to turn it your color. Fly over your rival's flowers to steal them back.

A golden flower pops up now and then. Grab it to color a whole patch of flowers around it in one go.

When the 60 seconds are up, the bee with the most flowers wins.

Honey (pink): W A S D

Buzz (blue): arrow keys

On a phone, use the two joysticks at the bottom: pink on the left, blue on the right.
