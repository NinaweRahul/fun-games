Chain Reaction

Fill cells until they blow. Wipe out your rival's orbs to win.

How to play

Take turns tapping a cell to add one orb. You can play in an empty cell or in a cell that already holds your color.

A cell explodes when its orb count equals its neighbors: 2 in corners, 3 on edges, 4 in the middle. It sends one orb to each neighbor and turns them to your color. That can set off chain reactions.

Wipe out every orb of your rival to win.

Cyan goes first, then Orange. Playing on one screen? Pass the device back and forth.
