🐝 BUMBLE DUEL

Two bees. One garden. Fly in the direction you're facing, and land a sting to win.

How to Play

Every bee uses the same controls. In Single Player you fly the honey bee against a wild bee. In Multiplayer, you and a friend each control your own bee from your own keyboard on your own device.

The Rules

Bees drift, braking is the only way to stop.

Stings last under a second.

Max 3 stings in the air at once.

Both bees get a moment to buzz off before stings can land.

One hit ends the duel.
